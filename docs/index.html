Ranked System and ELO Matchmaking

Ranked duels pair you with an opponent of similar rating through an ELO based matchmaking system. Win and you gain ELO points, lose and you drop some. Your rating decides which of the five divisions you sit in. A new ranked season starts every 3 months and all ranks reset, so everyone gets a clean run at the top.

How to Play

Where Am I? is designed to be picked up in seconds. No tutorial required.

  1. Open the app. Download from the App Store or Google Play, or visit whereamigame.app. No account, no registration.
  2. Watch the street walk video. Pay attention to architecture, vegetation, road signs, language on storefronts, weather and terrain.
  3. Drop your pin on the map. Tap the world map and place your pin where you think the video was filmed. Zoom in for precision.
  4. See your score. The closer your pin is to the real location, the more points you earn. The real location is revealed after each round with a short fact about the place.
  5. Compete and collect. Points feed the global leaderboard, correct country guesses earn badges, and landmark locations drop monument collectibles onto your world map.
  6. Enter ranked. ELO matchmaking finds an opponent at your level and every result moves your rating toward the next division.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free GeoGuessr?

Yes. Where Am I? GeoGuess Map Quiz is a free GeoGuessr alternative on iOS, Android and web. Every game mode is free, including ranked duels with ELO matchmaking, weekly tournaments and daily campaigns. There is no round limit, no subscription and no account requirement.

Is Where Am I? completely free?

Yes. The full game, including every video, every game mode, ranked play and all leaderboard features, is free with no subscription and no paywall. There are no premium tiers. The app is supported by optional in app cosmetic items, and none of them affect gameplay.

How does the ranked system work?

Ranked duels use ELO based matchmaking to pair you with a player of similar rating. Winning a competitive match adds ELO points, losing subtracts them, and your total rating places you in one of five divisions: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Master or Champion. A new ranked season begins every 3 months and all ranks reset.

What are the five divisions in Where Am I?

Bronze, Silver, Gold, Master and Champion. Bronze is the entry division for new ranked players and Champion is the top division. You move between them by gaining or losing ELO points in competitive matches.

How often do ranked seasons reset?

Every 3 months. When a season ends all ranks reset, so every player starts the new season on equal footing and has a fresh shot at Champion.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play immediately after installing the app or opening whereamigame.app. No email address, no password and no account creation of any kind is required to reach any game mode.

How is this different from GeoGuessr?

GeoGuessr limits free users to a small number of rounds per day and requires an account. Where Am I? has no round limits and requires no account. The bigger difference is format: GeoGuessr uses static Google Street View panoramas, while Where Am I? uses real street walk videos, which give you motion, sound and far more contextual clues. Ranked ELO matchmaking is also free here rather than being tied to a paid plan.

What makes street walk videos better than static Street View images?

Static images can be reverse searched with Google Lens and similar tools, which breaks competitive integrity, especially in ranked play. Videos cannot be reverse searched the same way. Walking footage also shows how a place behaves: traffic pace, ambient sound, how buildings are spaced, how people dress. A single frozen frame carries none of that.

Is there a web version, or is it mobile only?

Both. whereamigame.app is a full browser based version that works on desktop and mobile. The native iOS and Android apps add push notifications for daily campaigns and offline badge tracking.

When do weekly tournaments run?

Every week from Friday to Monday. You compete in groups across three days of challenges against players worldwide, and the final rankings award exclusive rewards.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download from the App Store for iPhone and iPad, or from Google Play for Android phones and tablets.

What countries are covered?

Videos currently span over 80 countries across all seven continents, and new locations are added regularly. The game tracks which countries you have guessed correctly and awards a badge for each one.
