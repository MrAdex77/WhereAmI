## Why Where Am I?

Most free GeoGuessr alternatives drop you into a static 360° image and ask you to guess. **Where Am I?** is different: you watch an actual street-walk video — a real person walking through a city, village, or landscape — before pinning your answer. That moving context makes the game far more immersive and far harder to cheat with reverse-image-search tools.

Key reasons players switch from GeoGuessr:

- **GeoGuessr now requires a paid subscription** for unlimited rounds. Where Am I? is free with no round limits.
- **GeoGuessr requires an account.** Where Am I? lets you play instantly with zero sign-up friction.
- **GeoGuessr is browser-only.** Where Am I? is a native iOS and Android app built for mobile-first play.
- **Static Street View images can be reverse-searched.** Our street-walk videos cannot.

## How to Play

Where Am I? is designed to be picked up in seconds — no tutorial required.

Download from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/where-am-i-explore-the-world/id6657987209) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adex77.WhereAmI), or visit [whereamigame.app](https://whereamigame.app/). No account, no registration.

**Step 2 — Watch the street-walk video**
A real walking video from somewhere in the world plays automatically. Pay attention to architecture, vegetation, road signs, language on storefronts, weather, and terrain.

**Step 3 — Drop your pin on the map**
When you're ready, tap the world map and place your pin where you think the video was filmed. Zoom in for precision.

**Step 4 — See your score**
The closer your pin to the actual location, the more points you earn. Perfect guesses earn maximum score and bonus XP. After each round, the real location is revealed with a short fact about the place.

**Step 5 — Compete and collect**
Your points feed into the global leaderboard. Correct country guesses unlock country badges. Confirmed landmark locations drop monument collectibles onto your personal world map.

## FAQ

**Is Where Am I? completely free?**
Yes. The full game — all videos, all game modes, all leaderboard features — is free with no subscription and no paywall. There are no premium tiers. The app is supported by optional in-app cosmetic items, but none affect gameplay.

**Do I need an account to play?**
No. You can play immediately after installing the app or opening whereamigame.app. No email address, no password, no account creation of any kind is required to access any game mode.

**How is this different from GeoGuessr?**
GeoGuessr now limits free users to a small number of rounds per day and requires an account. Where Am I? has no round limits and requires no account. The bigger difference is the format: GeoGuessr uses static Google Street View panoramas, while Where Am I? uses real street-walk videos — moving footage that gives you far more contextual clues.

**What makes street-walk videos better than static Street View images?**
Static images can be reverse-searched using Google Lens or similar tools, which breaks competitive integrity. Videos cannot be reverse-searched. More importantly, walking footage reveals how a place _feels_ — the pace of traffic, ambient sounds, how buildings are spaced, how people dress — contextual information that a single frozen frame cannot convey.

**Is there a web version, or is it mobile only?**
Both. [whereamigame.app](https://whereamigame.app/) is a full browser-based version that works on desktop and mobile. The native iOS and Android apps offer additional features like push notifications for daily campaigns and offline badge tracking.

**Does it work on iPhone and Android?**
Yes. Download from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/where-am-i-explore-the-world/id6657987209) for iPhone and iPad, or from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.adex77.WhereAmI) for Android phones and tablets.

**How does multiplayer work?**
In Multiplayer Duels, you and your opponent watch the same street-walk video at the same time. A timer counts down and both players pin their guesses simultaneously. The player whose pin is closest to the actual location wins the duel and earns more XP. You can challenge a specific friend using a duel code or be matched with a random player of similar skill.

**Is this appropriate for students and classrooms?**
Yes. Where Am I? is used by geography teachers and self-learners of all ages. The video-based format is especially effective for building genuine geographic intuition rather than memorization. Daily campaigns can be aligned with specific regions or themes that match curriculum topics.

**Can I submit my own walking videos?**
Yes — visit [whereamigame.app](https://whereamigame.app/) and tap "Submit Video" to contribute street-walk footage from your city or travels. Accepted videos become part of the global rotation and credit you as a contributor.

**What countries are covered?**
Videos currently span over 80 countries across all seven continents. New videos are added weekly. The game tracks which countries you have guessed correctly and awards a badge for each one.
